Terms of sale

The terms that apply when you buy a planning template from Vendoor Connect.

Last updated 18 October 2026

Who you're buying from

Templates on vendoorconnect.com are sold by Vendoor Connect. Contact: vendoor.templates@gmail.com. Payments are processed securely by Paystack, and "Vendoor Connect" appears on your receipt. We are responsible for your order, its delivery, customer support and any refunds.

What you're buying

Each template is a digital planning file, described on its page with its price in Naira (₦). Prices include any applicable charges; there are no delivery fees. We may change prices, but the price you pay is the one shown when you check out.

Payment

You can pay by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack. Your order is confirmed once Paystack confirms the payment.

Delivery and refunds

Templates are delivered digitally; see our delivery policy. Because they are digital downloads, change-of-mind purchases are handled by exchange or store credit, not cash refunds; see our refund policy.

How you can use your template

Your purchase gives you a personal licence to use and print the template for your own celebration and planning. You may share printed copies with your family, planning committee and vendors for that event. You may not resell, share the file publicly, or offer it as your own product. The designs remain the property of Vendoor Connect.

Reviews and vendor listings

Reviews must describe your own genuine experience. We check reviews before publishing and may decline or remove ones that are fake, abusive or off-topic. Vendors are independent businesses: we don't employ them or guarantee their work, and any booking or payment you make with a vendor is between you and them.

How reviews are checked and disputed

Every review is checked by us before it appears. Vendors can reply publicly to any review. If a vendor believes a review breaks these rules (for example, the reviewer was never their client), they can dispute it from their dashboard. We then look into it, which may include asking the reviewer or the vendor for details such as a booking confirmation. The review stays visible while we look into it, and we remove it only if it breaks these rules. No one can pay to remove, hide or change a review.

Vendor memberships and sponsored placements

Basic vendor listings are free. Vendors can choose to buy optional extras, which we agree with them by email before any payment:

  • Founding Vendor Membership (₦30,000 for 12 months, limited to the first 50 businesses): a Founding Vendor badge, placement ahead of standard listings in directory results, eligibility for homepage features rotated among founding members (not a permanent homepage spot), and one promotional feature on our Instagram. The 12 months start on the day we switch the membership on.
  • Sponsored placements (Homepage Spotlight, ₦15,000 per month; Website + Instagram Feature, ₦25,000 per month): extra visibility for the agreed period, always labelled as Sponsored. We offer a limited number each month.

Memberships and placements don't renew automatically. If we can't deliver something you've paid for, we'll reschedule it with you or refund that part. Paying for a membership or placement never affects reviews. We may decline or end a membership or placement for a business that misleads clients or breaks these terms, refunding any unused portion.

Questions and complaints

Email vendoor.templates@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
