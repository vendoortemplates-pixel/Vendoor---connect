About Vendoor Connect

Your trusted destination for planning life's most meaningful celebrations. Discover trusted vendors, stay organized with beautifully designed planning tools, share honest reviews, and celebrate every milestone with confidence.

Our Story

Why Vendoor Connect Exists

Nigeria doesn't have a real platform where people can find event vendors and read honest, real time reviews before booking. Most of us are stuck scrolling social media, hoping a caption or a comment section tells us the truth. It shouldn't be this hard, and it's the gap we're building Vendoor Connect to close.

The goal is simple. One place where vendors and clients actually find each other, where reviews are real, and where planning any celebration, from an introduction ceremony to a bridal shower to a full white wedding, feels less overwhelming and more exciting.

We're starting with our templates marketplace, self-guided planning templates for couples, bridal parties, and anyone organizing a celebration in Nigeria. It's the first step toward the full vendor discovery and review platform we're building, and every template is designed to be simple enough to pick up and start using in one sitting.
