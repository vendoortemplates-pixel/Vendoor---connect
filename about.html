Our Story

It Started With a Love for Celebrations

Vendoor Connect was born from a genuine love for event planning and a desire to make the experience better for everyone.

For years, I found myself being the person friends and family turned to when planning celebrations in Nigeria. From traditional weddings and birthdays to bridal showers and intimate gatherings, I was always happy to recommend vendors, share ideas, or help navigate the planning process. What started as a personal passion gradually became something much more meaningful.

Through these experiences, I recognized a gap in Nigeria's events industry. Finding reliable vendors often meant relying on social media, word of mouth, or recommendations that didn't always tell the full story. I believed there had to be a better way to connect people with the right vendors while making the planning process simpler and more transparent.

That belief became Vendoor Connect.

We're building a platform where vendors can showcase their businesses, clients can share honest experiences, and future customers can make informed decisions with confidence. Alongside this, we're creating thoughtfully designed, customizable planning templates to bring more structure, ease, and enjoyment to celebrations of every kind.

My vision for Vendoor Connect goes beyond simplifying event planning. It's about contributing to a more transparent, accountable, and connected events industry in Nigeria, where great vendors are recognized, genuine experiences matter, and every celebration begins with confidence.

Because I believe the experience of planning something beautiful should be just as memorable as the celebration itself.

Welcome to Vendoor Connect. Thoughtfully created for every celebration.
