Event Liaison

Your advocate, from the first call to the last dance

We manage the vendors, the appointments and the follow-ups, so you can stay present and enjoy every moment.

Book a consultation See what's included
The role

What does an event liaison do?

An event liaison is your dedicated point of contact, advocate and coordinator, throughout the planning process and on the day itself. We work alongside your planner, venue and vendors, but our focus is always you.

Instead of chasing replies, juggling WhatsApp threads and negotiating on your own, you have one trusted person keeping everything moving and making sure your wishes are heard.

  • I

    Your advocate

    We speak up for you with vendors, the planner and everyone involved, so your vision and budget are respected.

  • II

    Your scheduler

    We arrange every vendor appointment, consultation, tasting and fitting, and handle the follow-ups.

  • III

    Your negotiator

    We compare quotes and negotiate on your behalf for fair prices and clear terms.

Services

Support before the big day, and on it

Choose one service or combine both. We shape every booking around your celebration.

Before the wedding

Pre-Wedding Liaison Service

Planning support, coordination and advocacy, from the first enquiry to the final week.

  • One central point of contact between you, your planner, venue, vendors and family representatives
  • Scheduling all vendor enquiries, appointments, consultations and follow-ups
  • Comparing vendors and quotes, and negotiating on your behalf
  • Tracking deadlines, outstanding items and agreed next steps
  • Coordinating aso-ebi, beauty and styling, accommodation and transport
  • Support with logistics for guests travelling to Nigeria
  • Attending planning meetings and vendor consultations with you
  • Spotting problems early and resolving them with the right people

Pricing by consultation

On the day

Wedding Day Package

A dedicated liaison team by your side, so you never have to manage a thing.

  • A liaison team working alongside your planner and vendors, focused on you
  • Support through getting ready, photos, departure, arrival and every key moment
  • Managing your personal timeline and movements throughout the day
  • Coordinating photo timing, attire, makeup, gele and transport
  • Handling questions from vendors, family and the wedding party, so they don't reach you
  • Making sure you, and your closest family and friends, are fed, comfortable and looked after
  • Resolving issues quietly with the planner or vendor before they become your problem

Why both

Why you need a liaison and a planner

Your planner runs the event. Your liaison looks after you. Together, nothing is missed.

Your planner

  • Designs and produces the whole event
  • Manages décor, the programme and the run of show
  • Directs vendors and the venue on the day
  • Is responsible for the event as a whole

Your liaison

  • Represents you and your family's wishes
  • Schedules your vendor appointments and follow-ups
  • Negotiates with vendors on your behalf
  • Stays by your side on the day, focused only on you

One point of contact

Time back

Perfect from abroad

Present on the day
