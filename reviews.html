Trust & transparency

How reviews work on Vendoor Connect

Checked before publishing

Our team reads every review before it goes live and declines anything fake, abusive or off-topic.

Vendors can reply

Every vendor can respond publicly, so you hear both sides of the story.

Fair disputes

If a vendor believes a review breaks our rules, they can dispute it. We look into it, and the review stays up unless it does.

Never for sale

No one can pay to remove, hide or change a review. Sponsored vendors are always clearly labelled.
