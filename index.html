Hi, I'm Ganiat Asuni.

For years I was the person friends and family called when they were planning a wedding, a birthday or a bridal shower in Nigeria. Who's a good caterer? Can you trust this decorator? Is that price fair?

Too often the honest answers lived in WhatsApp chats and word of mouth. So I built Vendoor Connect: a place to find vendors other clients actually recommend, planners made for our traditions, and, when you need it, someone in your corner to handle the vendors for you.
