Refund policy

How refunds work for our digital planning templates.

Last updated 17 October 2026

Our templates are digital

Every Vendoor Connect planning template is a digital download. Because a downloaded file can't be returned, we don't give cash refunds for a change of mind. Instead, we offer an exchange or store credit. Please read the template description and use the Live Preview before you buy.

Exchange or store credit

If you bought the wrong template or it isn't right for your celebration, email us within 7 days of your purchase and choose one of these:

  • Exchange: we email you a different template of the same or lower price. If the one you want costs more, we'll send you a payment link for the difference.
  • Store credit: we give you credit for the full amount you paid, to use on any template within 6 months. To use it, email us with the template you'd like and we'll send it to you.

Each order can be exchanged or credited once.

When you get your money back

You'll get a full refund to your card or bank account if:

  • you paid but we couldn't deliver your template within 24 hours of payment;
  • the file is damaged, won't open or is missing pages, and we can't send you a working copy within 2 business days; or
  • you were charged more than once for the same order.

Refunds are paid back through Paystack and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

How to ask

Email vendoor.templates@gmail.com with your order reference (it starts with VC- and is on your Paystack receipt and the confirmation page), the email you paid with, and what you'd like: an exchange, store credit, or help with a file that won't work. We reply within 2 business days.

Custom templates

For custom templates we agree the details, price and any deposit with you in writing before work begins. If you cancel before we start designing, you get a full refund. Once design work has begun, refunds are agreed case by case based on the work completed.

Vendor listings

Listing a business in our vendor directory is free, so there is nothing to refund. Payments you make directly to a vendor you found on Vendoor Connect are between you and that vendor.
