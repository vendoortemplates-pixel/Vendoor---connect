Refund policy

How refunds work for our digital planning templates.

Last updated 17 October 2026

Our templates are digital

Every Vendoor Connect planning template is a digital file. Once a file has been downloaded or emailed to you it can't be returned, so we don't offer refunds simply for a change of mind. Please read the template description and use the Live Preview before you buy.

When we will refund or replace

We will give you a full refund, or a corrected file if you prefer, when:

  • you paid but did not receive your template within 24 hours of payment and we can't deliver it;
  • the file is damaged, won't open, or is missing pages; or
  • you received a different template from the one you paid for, or you were charged more than once for the same order.

How to ask for a refund

Email vendoor.templates@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase. Include your order reference (it starts with VC- and is on your Paystack receipt and the confirmation page), the email you paid with, and a short description of the problem. A screenshot helps if a file won't open.

We reply within 2 business days. If a fix is possible we'll send a corrected file first; if you'd still rather have your money back, or we can't fix it, we refund you in full.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the card or bank account you paid with, through Paystack. Depending on your bank, the money usually appears within 5 to 10 business days.

Custom templates

For custom templates we agree the details, price and any deposit with you in writing before work begins. If you cancel before we start designing, you get a full refund. Once design work has begun, refunds are agreed case by case based on the work completed.

Vendor listings

Listing a business in our vendor directory is free, so there is nothing to refund. Payments you make directly to a vendor you found on Vendoor Connect are between you and that vendor.
