Questions vendors ask

Do I need a membership to be listed?

No. Basic listings are free and stay free. Memberships and sponsored placements are optional extras for businesses that want more visibility.

Can I pay to remove or hide a negative review?

No, never. If you believe a review breaks our guidelines (for example, the person was never your client), you can dispute it from your dashboard and we'll look into it. You can also reply publicly to any review.

Will I always be on the homepage as a founding vendor?

Founding members are eligible for homepage features, which we rotate fairly among members. A permanent homepage spot isn't included. If you'd like guaranteed homepage time, ask about a Homepage Spotlight.

When does my 12 months start?

On the day your membership is switched on, not the day you enquire.

How do I pay?

Once the details are confirmed, we'll send you a secure payment link or bank transfer details. There are no automatic renewals or surprise charges.

What if a placement can't go ahead?

If we can't deliver a placement you've paid for, we'll reschedule it with you or refund that part. Details are in our terms.
